# Flashcards - Modernization

1. When does strangler beat big bang? When a critical system must evolve in parts.
2. When is replatforming sufficient? When managed services reduce operations without a deep redesign.
3. When is refactoring justified? When scale, agility, resilience or cost requires a new design.
4. When should you use SQS? For buffering, retries and producer-consumer decoupling.
5. When should you use EventBridge? For domain events and rule-based routing.
6. Where is Lambda strong? Short event-driven tasks, automatic scaling and low operational effort.
7. When do containers win? Custom runtimes, specific dependencies or long-running processes.
8. When does EKS make sense? With Kubernetes maturity and a clear need for its ecosystem.
9. When does Fargate help? When you want containers without managing the underlying instances.
10. What is a common pitfall? Changing technology without reducing coupling, risk or lead time.
