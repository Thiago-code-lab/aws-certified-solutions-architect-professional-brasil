# Official References

## Essential reading

- [Strangler fig pattern](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/strangler-fig.html) - incremental modernization; high priority.
- [AWS Lambda Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/lambda/) - event-driven serverless; medium priority.
- [Amazon ECS Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ecs/) - managed containers; medium priority.
- [Amazon EKS Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/eks/) - managed Kubernetes; medium priority.

## Further study

- [Amazon SQS Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sqs/) - queues, buffering and retries.
- [Amazon EventBridge Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/eventbridge/) - domain events and routing.
- [AWS Step Functions Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/step-functions/) - stateful workflows.

## Reference material

- [AWS Prescriptive Guidance migration strategy](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/large-migration-guide/migration-strategies.html) - migration and modernization context.
- [Serverless Applications Lens](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/) - serverless best practices.
- [Container Build Lens](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/container-build-lens/) - container best practices.

## Additional learning
