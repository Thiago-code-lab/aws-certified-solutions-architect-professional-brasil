# Referencias Oficiais

## Essencial

- [Strangler fig pattern](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/strangler-fig.html) - modernizacao incremental; prioridade alta.
- [AWS Lambda Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/lambda/) - serverless event-driven; prioridade media.
- [Amazon ECS Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ecs/) - containers gerenciados; prioridade media.
- [Amazon EKS Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/eks/) - Kubernetes gerenciado; prioridade media.

## Aprofundamento

- [Amazon SQS Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sqs/) - filas, buffer e retry.
- [Amazon EventBridge Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/eventbridge/) - eventos de dominio e roteamento.
- [AWS Step Functions Documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/step-functions/) - workflows com estado.

## Referencia

- [AWS Prescriptive Guidance migration strategy](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/migration-strategy/welcome.html) - contexto de migracao e modernizacao.
- [Serverless Applications Lens](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/serverless-applications-lens/) - boas praticas serverless.
- [Container Build Lens](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/container-build-lens/) - boas praticas de containers.

## Estudos Complementares

- [AWS Solutions Architect Associate](https://github.com/Thiago-code-lab/aws-certified-solutions-architect-associate-brasil) - opcional para Lambda, ECS, EKS, Fargate, SQS, SNS e EventBridge fundamentals.
- AI Practitioner removido: este modulo nao contem exemplo real de Bedrock, IA generativa ou workload AI/ML.
