# Final SAP-C02 Review (14 days)

## Block 1 (Days 1-4): organization, identity and networking

- Multi-account architecture, Control Tower, SCPs and cross-account access.
- Transit Gateway, Direct Connect, VPN, hybrid DNS and segmentation.

## Block 2 (Days 5-8): new solutions and continuity

- Multi-AZ, multi-Region, databases, storage, integration and serverless.
- Deployment strategies, rollback and automation.

## Block 3 (Days 9-11): continuous improvement

- Observability, security, DR, quotas, performance and FinOps.

## Block 4 (Days 12-14): migration, modernization and practice exams

- The 7 Rs, MGN, DMS, DataSync, migration waves and modernization.
- Review the error log and domain practice exams.

## Quick decision rule

1. Identify the dominant requirement.
2. Eliminate alternatives that violate security, RTO/RPO, governance or cost requirements.
3. Compare operational complexity among the remaining options.
4. Choose the most sustainable solution sufficient for the scenario.
