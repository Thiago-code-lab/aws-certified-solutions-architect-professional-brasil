# Lab - Migration Hub, Discovery and the 7 Rs

## Objective

Validate an architectural decision involving Migration Hub, Discovery and the 7 Rs at a small scale.

## Focused procedure

1. Draw the scenario and identify its dominant constraint.
2. List the services and quotas that need confirmation.
3. Model security, observability and rollback.
4. Run only the resources necessary to validate the primary behavior.
5. Record cost, risk and evidence before cleaning up the environment.
