# Cheatsheet - Well-Architected Framework and Advanced Trade-Offs

| Scenario cue | Likely decision | Exam caution |
|---|---|---|
| Governance or audit requirement | Prefer a controlled, verifiable design | Distinguish local permissions from organizational controls |
| Continuity requirement | Assess failover, replication and rollback | Verify high availability through testing |
| Performance requirement | Select the service for the access pattern | Validate quotas, latency and throughput |
| Cost requirement | Reduce waste while preserving reliability | Avoid savings that increase risk |
| Migration or modernization requirement | Choose the smallest change that meets the objective | Avoid unnecessary wholesale refactoring |
