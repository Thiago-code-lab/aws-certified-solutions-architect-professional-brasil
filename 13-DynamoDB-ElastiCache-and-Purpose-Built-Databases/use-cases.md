# Use Case - DynamoDB, ElastiCache and Purpose-Built Databases

## Scenario

An organization is reviewing an architecture involving DynamoDB, ElastiCache and Purpose-Built Databases. The solution must maintain continuous operations, security and controlled costs.

## Recommended decision

Choose the option that addresses the dominant requirement with automation, observability and clear controls. The justification must connect the technical design to business risk.

## Why it works

- Reduces manual operations.
- Preserves traceability.
- Maintains a path for rollback or further evolution.
- Avoids complexity without an explicit requirement.
