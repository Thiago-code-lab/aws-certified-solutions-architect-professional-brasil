# Referencias Oficiais

## Essencial

- [AWS Cost Management](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/) - ponto de entrada para ferramentas de custo; prioridade alta.
- [AWS Cost Explorer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/ce-what-is.html) - analise de tendencias; prioridade alta.
- [AWS Budgets](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/budgets-managing-costs.html) - alertas e limites; prioridade alta.

## Aprofundamento

- [Cost and Usage Report](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cur/latest/userguide/what-is-cur.html) - granularidade para showback e chargeback.
- [Savings Plans](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/savingsplans/latest/userguide/what-is-savings-plans.html) - compromisso flexivel de compute.
- [Reserved Instances](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSEC2/latest/UserGuide/ec2-reserved-instances.html) - compromisso especifico.
- [EC2 Spot Instances](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSEC2/latest/UserGuide/using-spot-instances.html) - workloads tolerantes a interrupcao.

## Referencia

- [S3 lifecycle](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/object-lifecycle-mgmt.html) - tiering e retencao.
- [Cost Optimization Pillar](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/) - boas praticas de custo.

## Estudos Complementares

- [AWS Cloud Practitioner](https://github.com/Thiago-code-lab/aws-certified-cloud-practitioner-brasil) - util para pricing, billing e conceitos basicos de custo.
- [AWS Solutions Architect Associate](https://github.com/Thiago-code-lab/aws-certified-solutions-architect-associate-brasil) - opcional para Auto Scaling, S3 lifecycle, NAT Gateway e fundamentos arquiteturais de custo.
