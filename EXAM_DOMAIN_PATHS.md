# SAP-C02 Exam Domain Paths

## Domain 1: Design Solutions for Organizational Complexity

Focus on multi-account architecture, governance, hybrid connectivity, centralized security, organizational resilience and cost visibility.

## Domain 2: Design for New Solutions

Focus on new architectures, deployment strategy, continuity, security, performance, reliability and cost optimization from the initial design.

## Domain 3: Continuous Improvement for Existing Solutions

Focus on operational improvement, security, performance, reliability, observability, automation, remediation and FinOps for existing environments.

## Domain 4: Accelerate Workload Migration and Modernization

Focus on assessment, the 7 Rs, wave planning, data and workload migration, and modernization with containers, serverless and managed services.
