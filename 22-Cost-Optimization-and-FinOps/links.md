# Official References

## Essential reading

- [AWS Cost Management](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/what-is-costmanagement.html) - entry point for cost tools; high priority.
- [AWS Cost Explorer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/ce-what-is.html) - trend analysis; high priority.
- [AWS Budgets](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/budgets-managing-costs.html) - budget thresholds, notifications and optional configured actions; high priority. A budget does not automatically impose a hard spending cap.

## Further study

- [Cost and Usage Report](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cur/latest/userguide/what-is-cur.html) - granular data for showback and chargeback.
- [Savings Plans](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/savingsplans/latest/userguide/what-is-savings-plans.html) - savings through a usage-spend commitment; eligible services and flexibility depend on plan type.
- [Reserved Instances](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSEC2/latest/UserGuide/ec2-reserved-instances.html) - discounts tied to instance attributes and term commitments; capacity benefits depend on scope.
- [EC2 Spot Instances](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSEC2/latest/UserGuide/using-spot-instances.html) - interruption-tolerant workloads.

## Reference material

- [S3 lifecycle](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/object-lifecycle-mgmt.html) - storage-class transitions and retention.
- [Cost Optimization Pillar](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/cost-optimization-pillar/) - cost best practices.

## Additional learning

- [AWS Cloud Practitioner](https://github.com/Thiago-code-lab/aws-certified-cloud-practitioner-brasil/tree/clf-c02-english) - useful for pricing, billing and basic cost concepts.
- [AWS Solutions Architect Associate](https://github.com/Thiago-code-lab/aws-certified-solutions-architect-associate-brasil/tree/saa-c03-english) - optional review of Auto Scaling, S3 lifecycle, NAT Gateway and architectural cost fundamentals.
