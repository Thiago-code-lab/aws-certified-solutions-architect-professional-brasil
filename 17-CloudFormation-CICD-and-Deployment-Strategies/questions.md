# Questions - CloudFormation, CI/CD and Deployment Strategies

## Question 1

A company needs to make a decision involving CloudFormation, CI/CD and Deployment Strategies in a scenario requiring security, continuous operations and predictable costs. Which approach is generally most appropriate?

A. Choose the most complex option even without an explicit requirement.
B. Prioritize the solution that meets requirements with lower operational risk and verifiable controls.
C. Use only manual operations to maintain flexibility.
D. Postpone observability and rollback.

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** SAP-C02 rewards sustainable decisions aligned with the dominant requirement and controlled risk.

## Question 2

Two alternatives are technically viable. What should determine the choice?

A. The number of services used.
B. Alignment with explicit requirements, quotas, cost and operations.
C. Personal preference for a service.
D. Less available documentation.

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Professional level tests trade-offs as well as service recognition.
